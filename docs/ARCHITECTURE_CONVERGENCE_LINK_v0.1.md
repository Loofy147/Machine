## Why

Machine owns machine-native substrate research and frontier evidence. It must contribute semantic results and bounded primitive candidates without turning research mechanisms into automatic production abstractions.

## What propagates from Machine

- bounded machine-native claims;
- experiment protocols and evidence receipts;
- resource/frontier contracts;
- explicit specification debt and open questions.

## What does not propagate automatically

- research branch code as production dependency;
- mechanism names as capability classes;
- finite experimental closure as a general theorem;
- unverified README/benchmark claims.

## Local obligation

Any candidate exported toward Open-System-One must carry repository + ref/commit + path + experiment/evidence provenance and a stated status.
