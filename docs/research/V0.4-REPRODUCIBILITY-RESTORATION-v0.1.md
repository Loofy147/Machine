## Remaining boundary

This restoration does not claim a new reproduction of the full --experiment all suite. The exhaustive weighted n=4 space, random n=4 schedules, and matched simple/multigraph sweep remain historical evidence until each is independently rerun and compared. Those outputs must not be described as newly reproduced by this workflow.

Likewise, passing the finite regression and n=3 enumeration supports only the stated positive-weight Dijkstra/UCS fixture and enumerated graph/schedule spaces. It does not prove the active guard for arbitrary graph sizes, arbitrary scheduling, or general heuristic search.
